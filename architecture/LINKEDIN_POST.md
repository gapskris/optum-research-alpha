### 🕵️ The Problem: The "Intelligence Gap"
Market analysts often wait weeks for clinical trial data to be "translated" into business strategy. In high-stakes industries like Healthcare and FinTech, those weeks represent lost billions.

### 🚀 The Solution: Optum Strategic Alpha
I built a full-stack Agentic Platform that automates the transition from **Raw Clinical Data** to **Executive Strategy** in minutes.

### 🛠️ How I built the Swarm:
1️⃣ **The Researcher**: Uses an autonomous **search-to-scrape loop** (Serper + Tavily + Scraper) to establish ground truth.
2️⃣ **The Outcomes Strategist**: Analyzes breakthroughs for Care Delivery (VBC) and patient impact.
3️⃣ **The Insight Consultant**: Maps clinical success to DaaS and AI productization opportunities.
4️⃣ **The Rx Specialist**: Predicts specialty drug spend and pharmacy benefit trajectory.
5️⃣ **The Financial Analyst**: Projects ROI, MLR delta, and outputs a capital recommendation (Invest / Watch / Avoid).

### ⚡ Technical Edge: The Adaptive Brain
*   **Hierarchical Failover**: Autonomous switching between Groq, Mistral, and Ollama Cloud to ensure 100% mission uptime.
*   **The Content Shield**: A custom proxy that strips JSON metadata, delivering 100% professional executive reports every time.
*   **Mission Control Dashboard**: Live SSE streaming of the agent swarm’s reasoning and "Chain of Thought."
